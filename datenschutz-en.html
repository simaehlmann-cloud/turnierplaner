2. Principle: no data collection

TourneyPro does not process any personal data on the provider's servers. The app works completely offline. There are no user accounts, no registration, no analytics or tracking services, no advertising and no sharing of data with third parties.

3. Data stored on the device

All entries remain exclusively in the local storage of the device. The following is stored:

  • Your teams: team and club name, strength rating and optionally squads with names and shirt numbers of the players
  • Optionally a crest or logo and a team photo per team, which you select or take yourself
  • Your tournaments: name, date, format, groups, schedule with kick-off times, pitches and referees, results and the tables and placings calculated from them
  • Your settings for language and appearance

This data is stored in the protected app directory using the browser technologies localStorage and IndexedDB. It is never transmitted to the provider. It is removed completely when you uninstall the app or delete the app data in the device settings. You can delete individual tournaments, teams, players and pictures at any time directly in the app.

4. Entering names and photos of third parties

When you enter names of players or add team photos, you process personal data under your own responsibility. As this data does not leave the device, nothing is transmitted to the provider. Please nevertheless observe the rules of your club or school. For photos of children and young people you generally need the consent of their parents or guardians. For minors it is advisable to use only first names, initials or shirt numbers.

Schedules, tables, pictures and backup files that you pass on via the share function contain the names and results entered; backup files also contain the stored pictures. If you share such content, you also pass on this information.

5. Camera and photo library

Only when you add a crest or team photo to a team does the app open the camera or the system photo picker at your request. The operating system may ask for permission once. The app only receives the single picture you select or take; it is scaled down and stored on the device only. The app does not access your other photos. You can revoke the permission at any time in the device settings.

6. Other device features

The app does not request permissions for microphone, contacts, location or telephony. While the match clock is running, the app asks the system to keep the screen on and plays signal sounds. When you share a schedule or export a file, the content is passed on via the share or save function of your device. Where it goes from there is entirely up to you.

7. Internet connections

TourneyPro contains all program parts in the installation package and does not establish any network connections on its own. This also applies to a web version of the app: only the app itself is loaded from the web server that provides it.

8. Distribution via an app store

When you download or buy the app via Google Play or the Apple App Store, the operator processes data independently (e.g. account, device, payment and time of download). The provider of this app has no influence on this. The privacy policy of the respective operator applies:

  • Google privacy policy
  • Apple privacy policy

9. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection under Art. 15 to 21 GDPR, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As the provider does not store any personal data, requests for access or erasure to the provider will usually have no effect. You can delete all data yourself in the app at any time.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy is updated as soon as features of the app that are relevant to data protection change. The current version is available in the app.
